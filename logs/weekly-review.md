# Weekly Reviews

Each Sunday evening, or whenever you ask for one, Claude summarizes the week from your logs and adds it to the top of this file. Each review covers the same items so weeks are easy to compare:

- **Weight:** the 7-day average and the change from last week (the average matters more than any single day)
- **Waist:** if measured
- **Gym:** sessions completed versus planned, and any lifts that went up
- **Cardio and yoga:** rides, pickleball, walks, and yoga sessions
- **Nutrition:** average calories, protein, fiber, and saturated fat on the days you logged
- **Treats:** beers, ice cream servings, sodas, fast-food meals
- **Money saved:** an estimate from skipped soda and fast food
- **Knee:** any flare-ups
- **One adjustment for next week**
