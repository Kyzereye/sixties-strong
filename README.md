## How tracking works

You don't need to edit these files yourself, though you can. The easy way is to open Claude in this folder and just tell it what happened, in plain language. For example:

- *"Gym today, session A. Leg press 140 for 12, 12, 11. Bench 80 for 12, 11, 10. Pulldown 80 for 12, 12, 12…"*
- *"Breakfast was the overnight oats. Lunch was a turkey sandwich and an apple. Dinner was two bowls of chili."*
- *"Rode the mountain bike, 13 miles, about 1,600 feet, took 2 hours 10 minutes. Knee felt fine."*
- *"Weighed 179.4 this morning."*
- *"Had 2 beers and some ice cream tonight."*

Claude records it in the right file, estimates the calories, protein, fiber, and saturated fat for food, and tells you how the day is adding up.

**Weigh-ins:** weigh yourself first thing in the morning, after using the bathroom and before eating, at least 3 times a week. Daily is even better. Day-to-day weight jumps around by 1 to 3 lb from water and salt, so we watch the **weekly average**, not single readings. Measure your waist at the belly button once a week if you can. Waist size often shrinks even when the scale stalls, because you're gaining muscle while losing fat.

**Weekly review:** each Sunday, ask Claude for your weekly review. It summarizes the week, adds it to `logs/weekly-review.md`, and suggests one adjustment for the coming week.

## The short version

1. **Lift every third day**, alternating Session A and Session B. Two days off, then lift. If you miss a day, go the next day, and never miss twice in a row.
2. **Ride the mountain bike once a week, on a weekday**, on the day right before a gym day. Play pickleball when you can. Do yoga twice a week, 20 to 25 minutes.
3. **Eat about 2,100 calories a day** (about 2,500 on big ride days), with **150+ g of protein**, **30+ g of fiber**, and **under 15 g of saturated fat**.
4. **Cook once a week** in the slow cooker so a homemade meal is always faster than a drive-thru.
5. **Beer and ice cream are planned, portioned, and logged**, not banned. **Soda and fast food stay mostly gone.**
6. **Log it, review it weekly, and adjust.**
