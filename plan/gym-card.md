# Gym Card

A quick reference to pull up on your phone at the gym. The full step-by-step form instructions for every exercise are in `training.md`, Section 5.

**Warm-up (8 min):** 5 min on the bike → 10 sit-to-stands → 10 hip hinges → arm circles and 15 band pull-aparts → one light set of 10 and one medium set of 5 before your first leg exercise and your first upper-body exercise.

**Effort:** stop each set when you have 1–2 good reps left. **Progress:** when you hit the top of the rep range on all 3 sets, add the smallest weight jump next time. **Lower every rep slowly, over 2–3 seconds.**

**What "pair" means:** two exercises are done back to back, then you rest. For example, do a set of side lateral raises, go straight to a set of triceps pushdowns without resting, then rest 60 seconds. Repeat until you've done 3 rounds. Because the two exercises work different muscles, one rests while the other works. It saves about 5 minutes. (Trainers call this a "superset.")

### How to do a face pull

1. Set a cable pulley at about face height and attach the **rope** (the same rope you use for triceps pushdowns).
2. Grab one end of the rope in each hand, thumbs pointing back toward you, and step back until your arms are straight. Stand tall with one foot slightly in front of the other.
3. Pull the rope toward your forehead, keeping your elbows high, at shoulder height or above. As the rope reaches your face, pull your hands apart so the rope ends finish beside your ears.
4. At the end you should look like you're flexing both biceps for a photo, and you should feel the backs of your shoulders and upper back squeeze.
5. Let the rope go back out slowly and repeat.

Keep it light. This exercise is about keeping your shoulders healthy, not about how much weight you move. If you have to lean back or jerk the rope, lower the weight.

## EXPRESS (30 min)

1. Leg press, 2 × 10–12
2. **Pair:** bench press *or* incline DB press, 2 × 8–12, with lat pulldown *or* seated row, 2 × 8–12
3. Romanian deadlift, 2 × 8–12
4. Plank, 2 × 30–45 seconds

---

**Cool-down (5 min, optional but worth it):** kneeling hip flexor stretch with a towel under your knee, 30 s per side; doorway chest stretch, 30 s per side; seated hamstring stretch, 30 s per side; child's pose, 1 min.

**After the gym:** a meal or shake with 35–45 g of protein within a couple of hours. Tell Claude your weights and reps so they get logged.
